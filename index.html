Clinical Summary

Patient initially diagnosed in March 2021 with ER+/PR+/HER2- invasive ductal carcinoma (T2N1M0), treated with curative-intent wide local excision, adjuvant ddAC-T chemotherapy, and adjuvant letrozole. Metastatic recurrence involving liver and bone was confirmed in August 2023. First-line palliative weekly paclitaxel was commenced and later dose-reduced due to peripheral neuropathy and neutropenia. The patient subsequently transitioned to second-line palliative systemic therapy with ribociclib and fulvestrant, which remains the current treatment. Most recent imaging demonstrates stable metastatic disease. Ongoing toxicities include Grade 3 neutropenia and Grade 2 fatigue.
